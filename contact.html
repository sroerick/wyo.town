§2

What engaging looks like

Small and medium business

We work with you at any stage of adoption. In practice it goes like this:

1

A conversation. Email back and forth until we both know what the problem is. No deck required.

2

A working session. We sit with your team — remotely or in Wyoming — and map where Super Intelligence actually fits your work.

3

Building alongside. We help your team tokenmax, spend sensibly, and build the thing. Your people stay in the loop at every step.

4

Sovereignty. What we build for you runs on your infrastructure. You own the stack, the data, and the keys when we are done.
