A message from the founder.

I was a pretty big SI skeptic until November of 2025. I got a contract gig at an SI company in a language I didn’t know, right as the models got good. I learned that the agents are very handy.

When GLM-5.3-flash came out — I realized the models were finally cheap enough to have assistants. Now, every SI company and their brother have SI assistants. But what they’re all missing is: how do you keep your assistants accountable? How do you organize the work? And do you really want all of it running on someone else’s servers, under someone else’s rules?

Wyo.town is a consulting firm with a suite of in-house tools for organizing agent teams, safely. We share our tools, and an ambitious youngster with an agent could likely deploy them himself. However, we believe that for you to build agents into your company, or to build a software factory, or to take advantage of Super Intelligence, you need to build it from the ground up to fit your organization. That’s what our tools do, and that’s what we do.

— roerick
